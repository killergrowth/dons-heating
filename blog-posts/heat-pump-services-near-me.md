## What Are Heat Pump Services?

Heat pump services cover everything your system needs to run efficiently and reliably:

- **Repairs** — diagnosing and fixing issues like refrigerant leaks, frozen coils, faulty reversing valves, or compressor problems
- **Installation** — replacing an aging system or upgrading to a heat pump for the first time
- **Maintenance** — seasonal tune-ups that extend equipment life and keep energy bills in check
- **Inspections** — diagnosing performance issues before they turn into expensive failures

Heat pumps are unique because they function as both heating and cooling systems. That versatility is great for homeowners — but it also means there's more to monitor and maintain.

---

## Signs You Need Heat Pump Service

Don't wait until your system quits completely. Call Don's if you notice any of these:

- **Weak airflow** from your vents in heating or cooling mode
- **System running constantly** without reaching your set temperature
- **Ice buildup** on the outdoor unit (outside of the normal defrost cycle)
- **Strange sounds** — grinding, squealing, or rattling from the unit
- **Unusually high utility bills** with no change in usage
- **System switching modes incorrectly** or failing to switch at all

Catching these issues early almost always means a faster fix and a lower repair bill.
