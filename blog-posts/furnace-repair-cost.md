## Average Furnace Repair Cost in 2026

Most furnace repairs fall somewhere in the **$150–$900 range**, though the full spectrum runs from $75 for a simple fix to $1,500+ for major component replacements.

Here's a quick breakdown by repair type:

| Repair | Typical Cost Range |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic / service call | $75–$150 |
| Ignitor replacement | $150–$300 |
| Flame sensor cleaning or replacement | $75–$200 |
| Blower motor replacement | $300–$700 |
| Heat exchanger replacement | $600–$1,500+ |
| Circuit board / control board | $400–$900 |
| Thermostat replacement | $100–$350 |
| Flue or exhaust repair | $150–$450 |
| Capacitor replacement | $100–$250 |

These are national ranges. What you actually pay in El Dorado, Hillsboro, or Emporia depends on your equipment, the part, and who you call.

## What Affects Furnace Repair Cost Most

### 1. The Part That Failed
Minor parts like flame sensors or igniters are cheap and fast to replace. A cracked heat exchanger is a different conversation — that's a major component, and if it's bad, replacement may cost more than the repair.

### 2. Age and Brand of Your Furnace
Older units often mean harder-to-source parts. A 20-year-old furnace that needs a control board may cost more to fix than a newer system — and if it's already at the end of its lifespan, your tech should tell you that straight.

### 3. Time of Call
Emergency service calls during nights, weekends, or holidays carry a premium in most markets. That's not unique to HVAC — it's how skilled trades work. Factor that in if your heat goes out at 11pm in January.

### 4. Labor Rate and Market
Rates in El Dorado, KS are generally lower than metro areas like Wichita, but that gap has narrowed as material and labor costs have risen industry-wide in 2025–2026.

## Common Signs Your Furnace Needs Repair (Not Replacement)

Not every furnace problem means you're buying a new system. These issues are typically repairable:

- **No heat or weak heat** — often an ignitor or flame sensor issue
- **Short cycling** (turns on and off quickly) — could be a dirty filter, clogged flue, or limit switch
- **Furnace won't turn on** — thermostat wiring, control board, or gas supply
- **Strange noises** — rattling, banging, or squealing usually point to blower motor or belt issues
- **Yellow pilot light** — should be blue; yellow can indicate a combustion problem

If your furnace is under 15 years old and the repair cost is under 50% of a new system's price, repair is usually the right call. If it's older and parts are failing repeatedly, your technician should walk you through the math on replacement.

## When to Stop Repairing and Start Replacing

A good technician will tell you when you're throwing money at a furnace that's on its way out. Watch for these signals:

- Repairs are needed every 1–2 seasons
- The system is 18+ years old
- Repair cost exceeds 50–60% of a new unit's price
- Heat exchanger is cracked (carbon monoxide risk — don't delay)
