## What Counts as an HVAC Emergency?

Not every HVAC issue is urgent, but some absolutely are. Here's when you should call immediately instead of waiting it out:

**Call right away if you're experiencing:**
- No heat during freezing temperatures
- No cooling during extreme summer heat
- Strange burning smells or electrical odors from your unit
- Carbon monoxide detector triggering near your furnace
- Refrigerant leaks (ice buildup on lines or the outdoor unit)
- Complete system failure with no response from the thermostat

If your system is making unusual sounds but still running, that's worth a same-day call — it often signals a failure that's hours away.
