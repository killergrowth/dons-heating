## What Does AC Installation Actually Cost in 2026?

If your air conditioner is on its last legs — or you're adding central air for the first time — the first question on your mind is probably: *what's this going to cost me?*

The honest answer: it depends. But that's not a dodge — there are a handful of real variables that swing the price significantly. This guide breaks down what Central Kansas homeowners should expect to pay for [air conditioning installation](/air-conditioning-installation/) in 2026, what drives costs up or down, and how to make sure you're getting a fair deal.

Nationally, homeowners pay between **$3,800 and $7,500** for a full central AC installation, with the average landing around **$5,200 to $5,800**. In Central Kansas — including El Dorado, Hillsboro, and Emporia — costs tend to track close to the national average, sometimes slightly lower due to lower labor rates than major metro areas.

These figures include equipment and labor. They assume a straightforward replacement on an existing system with ductwork in place.

## What Affects the Final Price

### 1. System Size (Tonnage)
AC units are sized in tons — not weight, but cooling capacity. A unit that's too small won't keep up with Kansas summers. Too large, and it short-cycles, wastes energy, and wears out faster. Proper sizing requires a load calculation based on your home's square footage, insulation, and layout. Skipping this step is one of the most common mistakes homeowners make when going with the cheapest quote.

### 2. SEER2 Rating (Efficiency)
Higher-efficiency units cost more upfront but lower your monthly electric bill. In 2026, minimum efficiency standards require **SEER2 ratings** on all new equipment. Entry-level systems start around 14 SEER2. Premium systems can reach 20+ SEER2. If you're in a home you plan to own for 10+ years, the efficiency upgrade often pays off.

### 3. Ductwork Condition
If your existing ductwork is leaking, undersized, or in poor shape, it'll need to be repaired or replaced before a new unit will perform correctly. Duct work adds **$1,000–$3,500+** depending on scope. This is why quotes vary so much — a company that skips the ductwork inspection is giving you an incomplete estimate.

### 4. New Installation vs. Replacement
Replacing an existing system is almost always cheaper than installing central air from scratch. New construction or homes converting from window units require full duct installation, electrical upgrades, and more labor. Expect to pay **$8,000–$15,000+** for a full new installation with ductwork.

### 5. Brand and Equipment Quality
Not all AC equipment is created equal. Brands like **Rheem and Mitsubishi** — both installed by Don's — are known for reliability and strong warranty coverage. Budget brands cost less upfront but often mean more service calls down the road.
