## Signs Your Furnace Needs Repair Right Now

Not sure if it's urgent? Here are the most common signs your furnace needs immediate attention:

- **No heat or weak airflow** — Your thermostat says one thing, your house says another
- **Furnace won't turn on** — Could be a ignition issue, thermostat failure, or a tripped safety switch
- **Unusual noises** — Banging, rattling, or squealing usually means a mechanical problem
- **Cycling on and off constantly** — Short cycling signals a dirty filter, a failing sensor, or something more serious
- **Yellow or orange pilot light** — Should be blue; a discolored flame can indicate a carbon monoxide risk
- **Spike in energy bills** — A struggling furnace works harder and costs more

If any of these sound familiar, don't wait. Our [furnace repair](/furnace-repair/) team can diagnose the issue and get your system running the same day in most cases.
