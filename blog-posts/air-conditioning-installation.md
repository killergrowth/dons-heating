## So You're Ready to Install a New AC — Here's What to Know

Getting a new air conditioning system is one of the bigger home investments you'll make. Done right, it'll keep your home comfortable for 15–20 years. Done wrong — wrong size, wrong equipment, wrong installer — and you'll pay for it in energy bills, breakdowns, and premature replacements.

This guide walks you through everything: how to size a system, what to expect during installation, what questions to ask your contractor, and what it really costs in El Dorado, KS and across Central Kansas.

---

## What Affects AC Size? (It's Not Just Square Footage)

The most common mistake homeowners make is assuming a bigger unit is better. It's not. An oversized AC cools the space too fast, shuts off before removing humidity, and cycles on and off constantly — which wears it out early and leaves your home clammy.

A properly sized system is calculated using a **Manual J load calculation**, which accounts for:

- Square footage and ceiling height
- Insulation quality and window size/placement
- Local climate (Central Kansas summers are no joke)
- Number of occupants
- Sun exposure and shade

For most Central Kansas homes, a 2–4 ton unit is the right range — but your home's specifics matter. Any contractor worth hiring will measure before recommending.

## Types of AC Systems: Which One Fits Your Home?

**Central Air (Split System)**
The most common setup. An outdoor condenser pairs with an indoor air handler connected to your existing ductwork. If you have a furnace and ducts, this is usually the most cost-effective path.

**Heat Pumps**
Heat pumps handle both heating and cooling in one system — a smart option for homes looking to reduce reliance on gas. Modern units from brands like **Mitsubishi** perform well even in Kansas winters. Learn more on our [heat pump services page](/heat-pump-services/).

**Ductless Mini-Splits**
No ductwork? No problem. Mini-splits are ideal for additions, older homes, or rooms that never get comfortable. They're more expensive upfront but highly efficient.

**Package Units**
All-in-one systems installed outside the home — common in manufactured homes or properties with limited indoor space.

## The Installation Process: Step by Step

Here's what a professional [air conditioning installation](/air-conditioning-installation/) actually looks like:

1. **Load calculation** — The technician assesses your home before recommending equipment.
2. **Equipment selection** — You choose a system (brand, efficiency rating, features) based on your budget and needs.
3. **Old system removal** — Existing equipment is disconnected and removed safely.
4. **Refrigerant lines and electrical** — New lines are run and connections made to your electrical panel.
5. **Indoor air handler installation** — Matched to the outdoor unit for proper efficiency.
6. **System startup and testing** — The tech verifies airflow, refrigerant charge, thermostat function, and checks for leaks.
7. **Walkthrough** — A good contractor explains the system, filters, and maintenance before they leave.

A standard residential installation takes **4–8 hours** depending on complexity. Replacing an existing system is typically faster than a first-time install.
