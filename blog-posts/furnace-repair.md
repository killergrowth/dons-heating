## Common Signs Your Furnace Needs Repair

Don't wait until your system completely fails. These are the warning signs most homeowners miss until it's too late:

### 1. No Heat — or Not Enough Heat
If your furnace is running but the air coming out feels lukewarm or cool, you likely have a problem with the heat exchanger, blower motor, or gas supply. Don't ignore it.

### 2. Strange Noises
- **Banging or popping** — usually a dirty burner or delayed ignition
- **Squealing or screeching** — belt or bearing issues in the blower
- **Rattling** — loose panels, ductwork, or internal components

### 3. Frequent Cycling
If your furnace turns on and off constantly without reaching the set temperature, this is called short cycling. Common causes include a clogged filter, faulty thermostat, or an oversized unit.

### 4. Yellow or Orange Flame
Your furnace burner flame should be blue. A yellow or orange flame can indicate a carbon monoxide issue — shut the system down and call immediately.

### 5. Rising Energy Bills
A furnace that's working harder than it should will show up in your utility bills before it shows up as a breakdown.

## What a Furnace Repair Typically Involves

When a technician arrives for a furnace repair call, here's what the process generally looks like:

1. **Diagnostic check** — The tech inspects the system, reads error codes, and identifies the root cause
2. **Part identification** — Common repairs include ignitors, flame sensors, heat exchangers, blower motors, and control boards
3. **Repair or replacement** — Most standard parts can be repaired same-day
4. **System test** — After repair, the system is tested through a full heat cycle to confirm it's working properly

## Furnace Maintenance Tips to Prevent Repairs

Most furnace breakdowns are preventable. Here's what to do before problems start:

- **Change your air filter every 1–3 months** — a clogged filter is the #1 cause of furnace failure
- **Schedule annual tune-ups** — ideally every fall before heating season hits
- **Keep vents clear** — don't block supply or return vents with furniture
- **Check your thermostat batteries** — a surprising number of "furnace problems" are dead batteries
- **Listen for new sounds** — catching a small issue early keeps repair costs low
