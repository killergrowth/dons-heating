## Common Furnace Problems We Fix

Most furnace failures come down to a handful of issues. Here's what our techs see most often:

### No Heat or Weak Heat
Could be a failed igniter, a tripped limit switch, or a dirty flame sensor. These are fast diagnoses when you know what you're looking at.

### Furnace Won't Turn On
Often a thermostat issue, a blown fuse, or a problem with the control board. Our [furnace repair guide](/blog/furnace-repair/) walks through the most common causes if you want to troubleshoot before calling.

### Short Cycling
Your furnace kicks on, runs for a minute, shuts off, repeats. This usually points to overheating — often caused by a clogged filter or a failing heat exchanger.

### Strange Noises
Banging, rattling, squealing — each sound points to something specific. Banging on startup often means delayed ignition. Rattling can signal loose panels or ductwork. Squealing usually means a blower motor bearing is going.

### Pilot Light or Ignition Issues
Older systems with standing pilots can have thermocouple failures. Newer systems with electronic ignition fail differently — intermittent startup, lockout codes, no spark.

If your furnace is showing any of these symptoms and you're not sure what's wrong, check out our full [furnace not turning on guide](/blog/furnace-not-turning-on/) for more detail before you decide whether to call.

- **Replace** if the system is 15–20+ years old, has had repeated failures, or the repair cost approaches what a new install would run.
