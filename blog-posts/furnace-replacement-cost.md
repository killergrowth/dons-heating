If your furnace is on its last leg, you've probably already started Googling prices — and found a dozen different numbers that don't agree with each other. That's normal. Furnace replacement cost depends on more variables than most homeowners expect, and national averages rarely reflect what you'll actually pay in Central Kansas.

Here's a real breakdown of what drives furnace replacement cost in 2027, so you can budget with confidence instead of guessing.

## What Determines Furnace Replacement Cost

Three things matter most: the size of your home, the efficiency rating you choose, and the complexity of the install. A straightforward swap — same size, same location, no ductwork changes — sits at the lower end of the range. Add in ductwork modifications, electrical upgrades, or a jump to a higher-efficiency unit, and the number climbs.

Fuel type plays a role too. Gas furnaces are generally more affordable to install than electric or dual-fuel systems, though electric units cost less upfront in some cases. If you're weighing your options, our guide on [gas vs electric furnaces](/blog/gas-vs-electric-furnace/) breaks down the tradeoffs in more depth.

**Typical cost ranges by efficiency tier:**

- **Standard efficiency (80% AFUE):** Lower upfront cost, solid for most Central Kansas homes
- **Mid-efficiency (90-95% AFUE):** Moderate increase, noticeably lower monthly bills
- **High-efficiency (96%+ AFUE):** Highest upfront investment, best long-term savings — especially relevant if you're also considering a [high efficiency furnace](/blog/high-efficiency-furnace-cost/)

## Signs It's Time to Replace, Not Repair

Before committing to replacement cost, make sure repair isn't the smarter move. The general rule of thumb: if your furnace is over 15 years old and the repair estimate exceeds 50% of a new unit's cost, replacement usually wins long-term. We cover the specific warning signs in detail in [Signs You Need a New Furnace](/blog/signs-you-need-a-new-furnace/) — worth a quick read before you make the call either way.

## What's Included in a Professional Installation

A furnace replacement cost quote should cover more than just the unit itself. Here's what a complete, done-right installation includes:

- Removal and disposal of your old furnace
- New unit, properly sized for your home's square footage (not just matched to the old one)
- Ductwork inspection and connection
- Electrical and gas line hookup
- Thermostat compatibility check
- Startup testing and safety inspection

Skipping any of these steps to save money upfront almost always costs more later — either in efficiency losses or in a system that doesn't last as long as it should.
