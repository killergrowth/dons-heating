If you're shopping for a new AC unit in 2027, you've probably already noticed the price tags vary wildly depending on who you ask. One quote says $4,000, another says $9,500, and you're left wondering what's actually going on. Here's the honest answer: both quotes could be right — it just depends on what's actually being installed and how.

At [Don's Heating & Air](/), we've been installing and repairing cooling systems across Central Kansas for over 65 years, so we've seen pricing shift with equipment standards, refrigerant regulations, and labor costs. Let's break down what a new AC unit actually costs in 2027 and what drives the number up or down.

## Average Cost of a New AC Unit in 2027

For most homes in the El Dorado, Hillsboro, and Emporia area, a full AC replacement — unit plus professional installation — typically runs between **$4,500 and $9,000**. That range covers a properly sized, mid-efficiency system installed by a licensed contractor, not just a bare unit dropped off at your curb.

A few things push that number around:

- **System size.** Bigger homes need bigger tonnage, and bigger tonnage costs more.
- **Efficiency rating (SEER2).** Higher-efficiency units cost more upfront but save money on utility bills over the system's life.
- **Brand and equipment tier.** We install Rheem systems because they balance reliability and value, but premium lines (like higher-tier Mitsubishi ductless systems) cost more.
- **Ductwork condition.** If your existing ductwork needs modification or repair, that's additional labor.
- **Refrigerant type.** Regulatory shifts away from older refrigerants have changed equipment costs industry-wide — this is one of the bigger reasons prices have moved since a few years ago.

If you're also weighing a [heat pump](/heat-pump-services/) instead of a traditional AC system, the cost conversation shifts a bit — heat pumps handle both heating and cooling, which can change the long-term value math depending on your home's setup.

## Why Installation Quality Matters More Than the Sticker Price

This is the part most homeowners don't think about until it's too late: a cheap unit installed poorly will cost you more in the long run than a properly sized unit installed correctly the first time.

Undersized systems run constantly and wear out faster. Oversized systems short-cycle, which wastes energy and shortens the equipment's life. Correct sizing requires an actual load calculation for your home — not a guess based on square footage alone.

That's one reason we offer [free on-site quotes](/get-a-quote/) instead of phone estimates. A technician needs to see your ductwork, your home's layout, and your existing system before any number means anything.

## What's Included in a Legitimate Quote

When you're comparing quotes for a [new air conditioning installation](/air-conditioning-installation/), make sure you're comparing apples to apples. A complete quote should include:

- The condenser unit and matching indoor coil
- Labor for removal of the old system and full installation
- Electrical and refrigerant line connections
- Permits where required
- A workmanship warranty separate from the manufacturer's equipment warranty

If a quote seems dramatically lower than others you've received, ask directly what's excluded. Sometimes it's a mismatched coil, sometimes it's a shorter warranty, and sometimes it's just a lowball number designed to get a signature before the "surprise" add-ons show up.

## Financing and Timing Considerations

AC systems rarely fail at a convenient time — usually it's the hottest week of summer when the old unit finally gives out. If you can plan ahead, scheduling a replacement in spring or fall often means faster scheduling and more time to compare options without the pressure of a 95-degree house.
